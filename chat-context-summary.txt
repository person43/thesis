- “Body-only import” means:
  - keep the paper’s main mathematical text
  - omit standalone title/author block
  - omit standalone abstract
  - omit standalone acknowledgements
  - omit standalone bibliography commands
- The `lop` import includes its appendix proof content, but not the paper's standalone bibliography commands or standalone `\appendix` switch.
- The `randomstrings` import includes its appendix proof content, but not the paper's standalone acknowledgements, bibliography commands, or standalone `\appendix` switch.
- To avoid cross-chapter collisions during the `randomstrings` import, its internal `\label{...}` keys and corresponding references were prefixed with `randomstrings:`.
- A thesis-level workaround was added in `thesis-body/preamble.tex` so that `\mathsf{...}` uses Computer Modern Sans; this fixes missing capital Greek glyphs in complexity classes such as `\mathsf{\Sigma_2^P}` and `\mathsf{\Pi_2^P}` under the thesis font stack.
